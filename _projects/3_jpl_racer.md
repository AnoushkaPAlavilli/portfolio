As part of the controls and planning team on the DARPA RACER project at NASA's Jet Propulsion
Laboratory, I did a research project on off-road vehicle dynamics modeling using visual terrain information. This work resulted in a paper publication {% cite gibson2025dynamics %}, and I presented our work at IEEE ICRA 2025 in Atlanta, GA.
